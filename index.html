The Experience

A journey through sweet challenges

It's not your typical puzzle game

Hoby Tales combines puzzle-solving with a sense of discovery. Levels are designed as small stories where players interact with the environment to move forward. Each level feels like you're discovering little stories instead of just checking boxes.

How To Play

Observe, interact, progress

  • Explore the Level

    Look at the environment to understand possible paths.

  • Interact with Objects

    Move, activate, or trigger elements in the scene.

  • Guide the Character

    Help the hero navigate safely.

  • Solve the Puzzle

    Find the correct sequence of actions.

  • Reach the Goal

    Complete the level to continue the journey.
